# Positioning: Northlight Studio

## For

Independent physiotherapy clinics and dental practices in England with 1–20 staff.

## Who struggle with

A slow, dated website that hides the booking link, so patients who already chose them still
phone, hesitate, or book elsewhere.

## We are

A small studio that builds fast, accessible, booking-first clinic websites in 14 days for a fixed
price.

## Unlike

DIY site builders (cheap, but the owner does the work and the result looks DIY), big agencies
(slow and priced for groups), and the friend who built the last site (no longer available).

## Because

- One 45-minute content call; we write the copy and the clinic approves it.
- Every page leads to the booking system they already use, so nothing changes for the front desk.
- Checked against WCAG 2.2 AA before launch.
- One fixed price: £1,800 per site, £900 deposit.

## Alternatives they use today

| Alternative | Why they stick with it | Our honest answer |
|---|---|---|
| The current site | It works, sort of | The audit shows what it costs them in friction |
| Wix / Squarespace themselves | Cheap, in their control | Fine if they have the time; we're for owners who don't |
| Their booking system's free page | Zero effort | It doesn't rank or explain treatments |
| A larger agency | Feels safe | Same outcome, longer timeline, higher price |
