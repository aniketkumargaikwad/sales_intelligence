# Messaging house: Northlight Studio

## Core message

Patients who already chose you shouldn't have to hunt for the booking button. We rebuild your
site in 14 days so every page leads to a booking.

## Pillars

1. **Booking-first.** Every page ends at the booking system the clinic already uses.
2. **Fast and accessible.** Quick on a phone, checked against WCAG 2.2 AA.
3. **No project pain.** One content call, we write it, they approve it, live in 14 days.
4. **Fixed price.** £1,800 per site, £900 deposit. No monthly contract.

## Proof we can use

None yet: `offer.proof` is empty, so we name no clients, show no case studies and quote no
results. What we *can* show is the prospect's own audit: their mobile score, their booking path,
their accessibility issues, each with the tool and date it came from.

## Things we never say

- Any price other than £1,800 per site and £900 deposit.
- "Free", "guaranteed", "urgent", "limited time", "#1".
- Any claim about rankings or more patients as a promise.
- Any client name, logo or testimonial until offer.proof lists it.
- Clinical claims on the clinic's behalf (GDC and HCPC advertising rules apply to them).

> I looked at your site on my phone: the booking link is three scrolls down on the homepage.

> Most of your patients have already decided to book by the time they reach your site. The site's only job is not to get in the way.

> Fixed price, £1,800 per site, live in 14 days after one content call.
