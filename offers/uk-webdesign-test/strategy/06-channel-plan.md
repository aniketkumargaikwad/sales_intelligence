# Channel plan: Northlight Studio

## Allowed channels

- **Email** to the clinic's published business address, only when the clinic is a limited
  company or LLP (PECR corporate subscriber), confirmed via Companies House. The message gives
  the sender's identity and postal address and has a one-click unsubscribe.
- **LinkedIn, manual only**: the founder sends a short connection note to the owner or practice
  manager, drafted by the system. No automation.

## Forbidden channels

- Cold calls, cold SMS and cold WhatsApp (never, for any offer).
- Cold email to sole traders or partnerships (PECR requires prior consent).
- Any automated LinkedIn action.

## Sequence outline

| Step | Day | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Email | Fixed frame. Slots: subject, greeting, one "scene" from the audit (e.g. the booking link three scrolls down). One link: the audit page. |
| 2 | 4 | Email (same thread) | Fixed follow-up: the one fix that would matter most, from the audit. |
| 3 | 11 | Email (same thread) | Fixed close-the-loop: "I'll stop here". Includes the price stated plainly. |
| Manual | after step 1 | LinkedIn | Connection note to the owner, only if they have a profile. |

Send windows: weekdays, 09:15–11:45 Europe/London. Any reply stops the sequence.
