# Personas: Northlight Studio

## Decision maker

**The owner-clinician.** A physio or principal dentist who owns the practice, still treats
patients most days, and does the business work in the evenings. Signs off any spend in this
range alone. Reads email between patients on a phone, so a long email loses them. Has been
burned by an agency before, or fears being.

## Day-to-day user

**The practice manager or receptionist.** Updates opening hours, adds a new clinician, fields
"how do I book?" calls that a better site would have prevented. Often the person who first reads
our email and forwards it. Wants something they can edit without calling a developer.

## What they care about

- Full diaries with the right patients (private treatments for dentists; self-pay and insured
  patients for physios).
- Not losing an evening to a website project.
- Looking as professional online as they are in the treatment room.
- Knowing what it costs up front: £1,800 per site, £900 deposit.

## What they fear

- A project that drags on for months, or an agency that disappears after launch.
- Being locked into a monthly contract or a platform they can't leave.
- Getting in trouble with the GDC/HCPC advertising rules through over-claiming copy.
- Losing their Google ranking during a switch.
