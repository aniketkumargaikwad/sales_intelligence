# Objection bank: Northlight Studio

## Objections

### "We already have a website."

- **What they usually mean:** I don't see what's wrong with it.
- **Honest answer:** Most clinics do. We'll send a one-page audit of the current site first:
  speed on a phone, how many taps it takes to book, and accessibility issues. If it's fine, we'll
  say so.
- **Proof or next step:** The audit (planned proof asset).
- **Source:** offer.yaml

### "We get patients through word of mouth."

- **What they usually mean:** Marketing isn't our problem.
- **Honest answer:** Word of mouth is the best source there is. Those patients still look you up
  before booking, and the site is where that referral turns into a booking or doesn't.
- **Proof or next step:** Show their booking path from a Google search to the booking page.
- **Source:** offer.yaml

### "We don't have time for a website project."

- **What they usually mean:** The last one ate my evenings.
- **Honest answer:** One 45-minute content call. We write the copy; you or your practice manager
  approve it. Live in 14 days.
- **Proof or next step:** Offer to book the content call at a time that suits the clinic.
- **Source:** offer.yaml

### "How much is it?"

- **What they usually mean:** Is this going to be a five-figure agency quote?
- **Honest answer:** £1,800 per site, with a £900 deposit. No monthly contract.
- **Proof or next step:** A short scope note listing what's included.
- **Source:** offer.yaml

### "Will we lose our Google ranking?"

- **What they usually mean:** I've heard horror stories.
- **Honest answer:** A rebuild done carelessly can. We keep existing page addresses or redirect
  them, keep the Google Business Profile link, and check Search Console after launch. We don't
  promise ranking gains.
- **Proof or next step:** The redirect checklist we follow.
- **Source:** (assumption) standard practice; confirm the studio's process.

### "We're locked into our booking system's website."

- **What they usually mean:** Changing feels risky.
- **Honest answer:** You keep your booking system; we link every page to it. Nothing changes
  for the front desk.
- **Proof or next step:** Name their booking system in the reply once we've seen it on their site.
- **Source:** offer.yaml differentiators

### "Send me something."

- **What they usually mean:** Not now, but not no.
- **Honest answer:** Send the audit, then one follow-up a week later. No more.
- **Source:** (assumption)
